> **AI voice interviews that screen every candidate and hand you a scored, shareable report.**
> Interview everyone. Read the report, not 200 resumes.

---

## 1. The problem

Hiring teams drown in early-stage screening:

- A single role can attract **hundreds of applicants**; resumes are a poor signal and easy to inflate.
- **First-round phone screens don't scale** — they eat recruiter hours, happen in narrow time windows, and quality varies by who's on the call.
- Strong candidates **drop off** during slow, back-and-forth scheduling.
- After all that effort, feedback is **inconsistent and hard to share** across the hiring team.

The result: recruiters spend their most expensive hours on the *first* filter, and good candidates slip through while weak ones advance.

## 2. The solution — VoxHire

VoxHire runs the **first-round interview automatically** with an AI voice interviewer, then produces a **structured, scored report** on every candidate — so your team spends time only on the people worth meeting.

Candidates click a link and have a real, adaptive voice conversation about their experience and skills. The AI greets them, asks role-specific questions, follows up on their answers, and at the end VoxHire generates a recruiter-ready report with a hire recommendation.

**No scheduling ping-pong. No recruiter on the call. Consistent evaluation for every candidate.**

## 3. How it works

1. **Post a role & add candidates.** Create a job; upload resumes one at a time or in bulk. VoxHire parses each résumé, de-duplicates candidates, and extracts skills.
2. **Send the link.** Candidates take an AI voice interview in their browser, on their own time — configured per role (skills, difficulty, type, duration).
3. **Read & share the report.** Every interview produces a scored report. Share it with a hiring manager via a one-click public link — no login, no PDF wrangling.

*(An optional AI phone/voice **screening** step can run before the interview — confirming availability, current/expected compensation, notice period, and intent — and auto-schedule the interview when the candidate is interested.)*

## 4. What you get

**AI voice interviews**
- Browser-based voice interview; the AI speaks first and adapts follow-ups to each answer.
- Fully configurable per candidate: role/title, tech stack & focus skills, difficulty, interview type, and duration.

**Automatic, structured reports**
- Per-skill ratings, communication / confidence / clarity scores, strengths & gaps.
- A clear **hire recommendation** (Strong Hire / Hire / Consider / Reject).
- **Resume-claim verification** — cross-checks what the candidate said against their résumé and flags each claim as verified, partial, or unsupported.
- Full transcript and an interview timeline.

**Shareable reports**
- One-click **public report link** (read-only, no login) to share with hiring managers; revocable any time. PII and transcript are kept private on shared links.

**Screening (optional)**
- AI voice screening that captures availability, compensation, notice period, and intent — and can auto-schedule the interview.

**Integrity & fairness**
- Every candidate gets the **same** structured interview and rubric.
- Built-in proctoring (tab-switching, fullscreen, multiple-display, dev-tools and copy/paste checks) with a transparent flag summary on the report. Candidates are told the rules up front.

**Resume intelligence**
- Single and bulk résumé parsing, skill extraction, duplicate detection, and job matching.

**Recruiting analytics**
- A dashboard with real metrics: candidates, interviews completed, completion rate, hiring funnel, and recruiter-hours saved.

**Built for teams**
- Multi-tenant with roles (org admin, recruiter); each organization's data is isolated.
