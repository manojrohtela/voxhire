# VoxHire — Marketing Video Kit (social media)

A ready-to-shoot script for a **60–75s** product video for LinkedIn / X / Instagram / YouTube Shorts,
plus a **30s cut**, a **shot list**, on-screen captions, and ready-to-paste posting copy.

Everything below is screen-recordable today from the live product + demo — no actors needed.

---

## A. The 60–75s main video

**Format:** screen recording + captions + voiceover (or captions-only with music — most social video is watched muted, so on-screen text is essential).

| Time | Visual | On-screen caption | Voiceover (optional) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Fast scroll through a giant pile of resumes / a packed inbox | **"200 applicants. One role."** | "Every open role, hundreds of resumes." |
| 0:03–0:09 | A calendar with endless screening-call slots; a tired recruiter | **"First-round screening doesn't scale."** | "First-round phone screens eat your week — and good people drop off waiting." |
| 0:09–0:14 | Clean cut to VoxHire logo / dashboard | **"Meet VoxHire."** | "VoxHire interviews every candidate for you." |
| 0:14–0:26 | Candidate clicks a link → browser interview → AI greets them, waveform animates, candidate answers | **"AI voice interviews — in the browser. The AI adapts to each answer."** | "Candidates click a link and have a real voice interview — tailored to the role's actual stack." |
| 0:26–0:40 | The report page builds: scores ring up, skill bars fill, recommendation badge appears | **"Then a scored report. Automatically."** | "You get a structured report — skills, communication, a hire recommendation, even résumé-claim verification." |
| 0:40–0:50 | Click **Share report** → copy link → open the public report in a clean tab | **"Share it with your team in one click. No login."** | "Share it with a hiring manager in one click." |
| 0:50–0:62 | Dashboard analytics: candidates, completion rate, recruiter-hours saved | **"Interview everyone. Read the report, not 200 resumes."** | "Interview everyone. Spend your time only on the people worth meeting." |
| 0:62–0:72 | VoxHire logo + URL + CTA | **"Try the live demo — no signup. → voxhire.heyagenthive.com"** | "Try the live demo, free, no signup." |

**End card:** logo · "AI voice interviews + scored reports" · URL · `[your handle]`.

## B. The 30s cut (for X / Reels / Shorts)

1. **0:00–0:04** — "200 applicants. One role." (resume pile)
2. **0:04–0:14** — VoxHire interview: candidate talking to the AI in-browser. Caption: *"AI interviews every candidate — in their own stack."*
3. **0:14–0:24** — Report builds + **Share link**. Caption: *"Get a scored, shareable report. Automatically."*
4. **0:24–0:30** — End card + CTA: *"Live demo, no signup → voxhire.heyagenthive.com"*

## C. Shot list (record these clips)

Record at 1080×1920 (vertical) for Reels/Shorts/TikTok, and 1920×1080 (landscape) for LinkedIn/YouTube. Capture each clean, then edit:

1. **Resume pile / inbox** — stock or a quick mock of a crowded applicant list.
2. **Interview start** — `/interview/test`: set a name + stack, hit start, capture the AI greeting + waveform + a candidate answer (record your own audio).
3. **Report reveal** — open a completed report; capture the score rings, skill bars, and recommendation badge. *(Use the demo org's completed candidates, e.g. the "Strong Hire" one.)*
4. **Share flow** — click **Share report**, copy the link, open the public report in an incognito tab.
5. **Analytics** — the demo dashboard metrics + hiring funnel.
6. **End card** — logo + URL (make in Canva/Figma).

> Tip: keep each cut ≤ 3s, use snappy zoom/cut transitions, and **burn in captions** — assume muted playback.

## D. Music & style

- Upbeat, modern, minimal (royalty-free: Epidemic Sound / Artlist / YouTube Audio Library).
- Brand color **indigo `#6C63FF`**; clean sans-serif captions; dark UI looks great on screen.
- Keep it fast — a cut every 2–3 seconds holds attention.
